# Geneoscopy

> Geneoscopy is a commercial-stage molecular diagnostics company in St. Louis, Missouri, focused on gastrointestinal health. It analyzes stool-derived eukaryotic RNA (seRNA) biomarkers, and its lead product ColoSense is a noninvasive multi-target stool RNA (mt-sRNA) colorectal cancer screening test that the FDA approved in May 2024 for average-risk adults aged 45 and older. Geneoscopy runs a CLIA-certified and CAP-accredited clinical laboratory. It is a diagnostics and laboratory company and publishes no public developer API, SDK, developer portal, or machine-readable API contract.
